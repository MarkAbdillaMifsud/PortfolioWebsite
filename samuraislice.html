Samurai Slice!

A short-session arcade game I built in Unity for Android. You play a retired samurai turned sushi chef, swiping through airborne ingredients to chase a high score.

Project metadata

Role
Solo Developer
Project

Browse the Unity source on GitHub

Scoring and risk

Slicing several ingredients in one swipe builds a combo and earns more points. I used that rule to give players a reason to wait for ingredients to line up. Missing three ingredients ends the run, and slicing a wasabi bomb ends it immediately. Players have to weigh a bigger combo against the risk of waiting or swiping through a hazard.

Pacing and feedback

The run starts with a slower pace so players can find their swipe rhythm. As it progresses, ingredients arrive faster and wasabi bombs become more likely. I exposed spawn timing and bomb probability as separate Unity curves so I could tune them independently.

An extra layer of music comes in when the swipe reaches the combo threshold, so players can hear the reward while keeping their eyes on the ingredients.
