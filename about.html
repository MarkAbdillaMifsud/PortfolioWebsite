About

I work across monetization, LiveOps and economy design for free-to-play strategy games. I partner with product, UX/UI, data and community teams to shape offers and events around how players progress, what they value and where they encounter friction.

On Supremacy: World War 3, my work covered the webshop, first-purchase experiments and seasonal LiveOps. For Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000, I focused on launch planning, including how to structure early offers and what to test. On Merge Dragons, my work involved LiveOps performance analysis and monetization strategy.

Expertise

Monetization & experimentation

Webshop presentation, first-purchase offers, A/B test design and evaluation.

LiveOps & player behaviour

Season planning, event rewards and participation in alliance competitions.

Game economy & systems design

Resource flows, crafting risk and balancing through economy simulation.

Tools and technologies include Machinations, Unity, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and Java.
